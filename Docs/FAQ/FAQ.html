Frequently Asked Questions

Here are the most common questions regarding the "In The Ward Of..." handbook series, target readership, and clinical methodology.

General Questions:

  1. Who is this handbook series intended for?

    It is specifically structured for 4th to 6th-year medical students, interns, and junior medical officers during clinical rotations.

  2. Does this replace standard textbooks?

    No, these handbooks are designed as high-yield quick references and revision companions for bedside shifts and OSCE preparations.

  3. How are the topics organized within each handbook?

    Each module follows a structured case-based approach: Definitions, Clinical Features, Diagnostic Criteria, Associated Conditions, Differential Diagnosis, and Step-by-Step Management.
